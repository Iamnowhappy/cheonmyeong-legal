Privacy Policy

천명연구소 (Cheonmyeong Institute)
Last updated: 2026-09-09

1. What we collect

This app only accesses OAuth access tokens for the developer's own connected social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads), obtained directly from each platform's official authorization flow. We do not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any other individual or third party.

2. How data is used

Access tokens are used exclusively to publish video content to the developer's own accounts via each platform's official API. Tokens are stored locally and are never transmitted to any third party.

3. Data retention

Tokens remain on the developer's local device until manually revoked or replaced. No data is retained on external servers operated by this app.

4. Third-party platforms

Use of connected platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads) is also governed by each platform's own privacy policy and terms.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: hssgchng@naver.com
